# Timed-transcript editor, not a studio

The job is to find whether something was said, then play or cite it. Describer does not record meetings, does not write specs, and does not cut or rearrange the Source. Edits correct the Transcript; the media stays immutable. Specs and recording stay in other tools.

**Considered options:** text-driven video editor (delete text, shorten media); in-app recorder; specs with linked quotes inside this app.
